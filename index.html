The Bridge Exchange

Same budget. A live market. One bridge. Load it until it breaks.

Every team starts with 1,000 credits. Premium materials are easy to build with, but expensive. Newspaper costs almost nothing, if you know how to make it strong. Expert tips are for sale, and every purchase moves the price for everyone else.

  1. Step 1

    Bid

    Buy materials and know-how on a market that reacts to every team’s demand.

  2. Step 2

    Build

    Premium and easy, or cheap and clever. Spend it now, or keep it for the score.

  3. Step 3

    Break

    Load it live in front of everyone. The highest Legend Score wins.

Legend Score

Span × Load × (1 + Unspent ÷ Budget)

Span times load measures the bending moment the bridge resists, so a long bridge and a strong one compete fairly. Every credit you don’t spend multiplies your result.
