The Bridge Exchange

Your mission

Build the bridge that wins the room.

The challenge

So does every other team, and you’re all shopping at the same market.

Every team gets 1,000 credits, and every purchase moves the price. Premium buys speed. Patience buys bargains. Newspaper costs almost nothing, if your team can make it hold.

Need an edge? Buy an expert tip. A proven blueprint. A spy card for a look at a rival’s plan. Or skip the market and strike a deal with the team next to you.

Then comes the moment everyone awaits. Your bridge on the rig, the sand going in, the whole room counting… Until it breaks.

The final score rewards how far you span, how much you carry, and how much money you kept.

Spend like an engineer. Save like a strategist

  1. Step 1

    Bid

    Buy materials and know-how on a market that reacts to every team’s demand.

  2. Step 2

    Build

    Premium and easy, or cheap and clever. Spend it now, or keep it for the score.

  3. Step 3

    Break

    Load it live in front of everyone. The highest Legend Score wins.

Legend Score

Span × Load × (1 + Unspent ÷ Budget)

Span times load measures the bending moment the bridge resists, so a long bridge and a strong one compete fairly. Every credit you don’t spend multiplies your result.
